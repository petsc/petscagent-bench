What one change buys, with its uncertainty

Each row is a pair of purple variants differing in exactly one factor, paired problem by problem and bootstrapped over problems. Pairing is what makes a seven-problem suite able to resolve anything at all: problem difficulty is the largest term in the data and it cancels inside a pair. r is the correlation between the two arms, so a row with low r is one where pairing bought little and the interval should be read as wide on purpose.

Every run, not the average

One mark per problem-run, one column per variant. The scores pile up at zero and again near the top with little between, so a mean sits in a gap no run occupies. The zeros are runs that threw before evaluation, so they count a crash rather than measure quality. Bars show the bootstrap 95% interval over problems.

Where the pipeline loses runs

Each stage as a share of all problem-runs in the current slice. The largest single loss is between compiled and executed, which is also the only stage where the harness stops and records an exception instead of a score.

The judge moves the score too

Mean composite by judge, for each variant. When the gap between judges on the same submissions rivals the gap between variants, no score means anything without naming its judge.

How the losses break down

Every problem-run in the current slice, split by what the harness recorded, one bar per variant. This answers what the grid above cannot: whether a change moved the kind of failure or only its rate. What the score costs

Mean tokens against mean composite, one point per variant. A scaffold that adds sub-agents buys its points with tokens, and this is the only view where that price is visible. Points up and to the left are better.

Buy problems, or buy runs?

Score variance split into the part that lives between problems and the part that lives between repeated runs of the same problem. Repeating a run only shrinks the second term, so when the first dominates, extra runs of the same suite buy almost nothing and the next unit of compute belongs on new problems. The projection is the half-width of a 95% interval on a variant mean, under the variance split measured here.
